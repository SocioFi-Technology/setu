01

Colour

Setu teal is the brand and the colour of anything final or confirmed. Marigold is reserved for the single key action on a screen (Collect payment, Admit, Sign). Violet always means "not final yet". Colour never carries meaning alone: every status, flag and bed state also has a text label and an icon.

1.1 Scales 50–900

Defined in OKLCH, exported as hex. The ratio under each swatch is contrast against white. Use 700+ for text on white; use 50–100 for subtle fills.

02

Typography

One stack serves both scripts: IBM Plex Sans for Latin (an Inter-class grotesque with true tabular figures) and Noto Sans Bengali for Bangla, which has the most complete conjunct coverage on Android. Bangla gets extra line-height at every size so matras, ref (র্) and below-base vowels (ু ূ ৃ) never clip. IBM Plex Mono is for IDs, codes and token names.

03

Numerals, money & dates

Numerals are a per-user setting, independent of language. Stored values are always Latin digits; conversion happens at display. Money uses South Asian grouping (thousand, lakh, crore) and always prints amount in words on receipts and invoices. Phone numbers, IDs and lab values use tabular figures. Helpers live in lib/setu-format.js.

05

Iconography

One set: Lucide line icons (2px stroke on a 24 grid), sized 16 / 20 / 24. Icons support a label; they never replace one. Navigation always shows the text. Icon-only buttons are allowed only in dense toolbars and must have a tooltip and an accessible name. No emoji.

07

Content style

7.1 Voice

  • Plain and respectful. Bangla uses the formal আপনি form and standard চলিত ভাষা. English uses short, active sentences.
  • Verb-first actions. "সংরক্ষণ করুন · Save", "পেমেন্ট নিন · Collect payment". Never "OK" or "Submit".
  • Clinical terms stay clinical. Test and drug names stay in English (CBC, Napa) inside Bangla sentences; they are not transliterated on staff screens. The patient app may add a Bangla gloss.
  • Say who and when. "ডা. ফারহানা ইসলাম স্বাক্ষর করেছেন · ২৯/০৯/২০২৬ ১০:৪২".
  • No blame, no alarm. Describe the state and the next step. Reserve "জরুরি · Urgent" for clinical urgency.
  • Patients read at a glance. Patient app copy targets class-8 reading level, one idea per sentence.

7.2 Error message pattern

What happened → why (if known) → what to do
