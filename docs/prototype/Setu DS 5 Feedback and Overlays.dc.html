Behaviour

  • Sign stays disabled until the AI draft is acknowledged and a 4–6 digit PIN is entered.
  • Unverified identity blocks signing and links to verification.
  • After Sign: the button shows a spinner and the content keeps its draft styling until the server confirms.
  • Offline: signing is unavailable; the draft stays on the device.
  • Signed items are corrected by amendment; the modal for an amendment adds a required reason field.
Try it: tick the checkbox, type any 4 digits, press Sign. Then tick "Simulate offline".

Toasts & inline alerts

Toasts (live)

Send SMS shows "Sending…" first and only turns into "Sent" when the gateway confirms (simulated 2 s). Errors stay until dismissed; success auto-hides after 6 s. Toasts are never the only record of an action.
