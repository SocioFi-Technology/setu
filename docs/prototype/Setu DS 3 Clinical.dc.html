Clinical components ক্লিনিক্যাল

The patient header, source badges and status pills carry the product rules: identity confidence is visible, every item shows who recorded it, drafts never look final, corrections keep the original, and colour always travels with a word and an icon.

Rules for every variant

  • Name in Bangla first, English beside it. Either can be missing; never both.
  • Allergy line is always visible, including billing and mobile. It is red only when allergies exist. "No known allergies" and "not recorded" are separate states.
  • Identity: verified, possible duplicate, or unverified. The last two add a strip that needs a decision.
  • Patient no. in mono; age in years + months (+ days under 1 year).
  • Collapsed on scroll to one line: name · age/sex · ID · allergy icon + count.

Draft vs signed

A draft carries hatch, dashed border and a watermark. Pressing Sign does not make it look final: it stays a draft until the server confirms.
