One platform for Bangladesh’s clinics, hospitals and their patients

A Bangla-first hospital and clinic management base (OPD, IPD, beds, nursing, billing, pharmacy, lab, OT, ER, owner dashboards) with a connected-care layer: bKash/Nagad payments, SMS delivery, QR-verified documents, a multi-chamber doctor app, a patient Health Passport, and consented record sharing between independent facilities.
